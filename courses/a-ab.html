P1.
\( f(x) = -3x^3+x^2+9x-3 \)

a) Factor completely by grouping two by two and then using the difference of squares formula from algebra:
\( f(x) = (1-3x) (x-\sqrt{3}) (x+\sqrt{3}) \)

b) The y-intercept is: \( (0,-3) \).
The x-intercepts are: \( (\pm\sqrt{3},0), (1/3, 0) \)

c) Sketch neatly by placing arrows on the positive end of the x- and y-axis. Indicate the four intercepts clearly.

d) The domain of \( h(x) = \frac{1}{g(x)} \) consists of all reals except the x-intercepts of \( g(x) \), since the denominator cannot be zero. Therefore the domain is all reals except for: \( \pm\sqrt{3}, 1/3 \).

Ignore the first by stating that no such x satisfies the equation, and find \( x = \frac{3\pi}{2} \) from the second possibility.
