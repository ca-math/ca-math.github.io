October 11, 2026 | Finding slope with the TI-84

OPTION 1: FROM THE GRAPH

1. Press [Y=] and enter your function (e.g., y = -3x + 5).
2. Press [GRAPH].
3. Press [2nd] then [TRACE] (the CALC menu).
4. Select dy/dx (option 6).
5. Type the x-value where you want to find the slope and press [ENTER].

OPTION 2: THE NDERIV COMMAND

1. Open the Math Menu: Press the [MATH] key on your calculator.

2. Select Option 8: Scroll down or press 8 to select 8:nDeriv( and press [ENTER].

3. Fill in the Arguments:
• In MathPrint mode (newer operating systems):
• Enter the variable (press [X,T,θ,n] for x).
• Type your function expression in the main box.
• Arrow over to x = and type the specific x-coordinate where you want to find the slope.
• In Classic mode (older operating systems):
• Enter the syntax: nDeriv(function, variable, value) separated by commas (e.g., nDeriv(x^2, x, 3)).

4. Calculate: Press [ENTER] to display the numerical derivative (slope) at that point.

Quiz A Problem 1
Find the equation of the normal line to the curve with equation \( f(x) = \sec x + \csc x \) at \( x = \pi/6 \).
The y-value: \( f(\pi/6) = \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3} + 2 \)
The slope: \( f'(\pi/6) = \frac{2-6\sqrt{3}}{3} \)
The slope of the normal line: \( \frac{3}{6\sqrt{3}-2} \)

Note: For the equation of the normal line we did not need to simplify. In other words, a point-slope form is fine.


Quiz A Problem 2
\( y = \cot x \)
The points on the curve where the tangent line is perpendicular to the line \( y = \frac{x}{2} \) have slope equal to -2, so we find and set the derivative equal to this number:
\( f'(x) = -\csc^2 x = -2 \rightarrow (\pi/4, -1), (\pi/4,1) \)


Quiz B Problem 1
Find the equation of the tangent line to \(y= \cot x + x \) at \( x=\pi/3 \).
The derivative and slope: \( dy/dx = -\csc^2 x + 1 \rightarrow \frac{dy}{dx}|_{x=\pi/3} = \frac{-1}{3} \)
The y-value: \(y(\pi/3) = \frac{\pi+\sqrt{3}}{3} \)
The equation of the tangent line is: \( y= \frac{-x}{3} +\frac{4\pi}{9} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\).

Note: For the equation of the line we did not need to simplify. In other words, a point-slope form is fine.


Quiz B Problem 2
