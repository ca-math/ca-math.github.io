P2.
The equation was: \( \csc^2x - \csc x - 2 = 0 \)
Factor the left side: \( (\csc x - 2) (\csc x + 1) = 0 \)
Consider both cases in the given domain ( \( [0,2\pi) \)).
Case 1: \( \csc x = 2 \rightarrow \sin x = \frac{1}{2} \)
Case 2: \( \csc x = -1 \rightarrow \sin x = -1 \)
Conclude with three solutions: \( x = \frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{5\pi}{6}, \frac{3\pi}{2} \).
